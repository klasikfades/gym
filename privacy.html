Your Gym Name (“we”, “us”) runs the membership system used at our front desk and on this website. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, email address and postal address.
  • Emergency contact: a name and phone number you give us, so we can reach someone if needed.
  • Membership records: the plan you are on, start and end dates, freezes and renewals.
  • Payments: amounts, dates, method and any reference number. We do not store card numbers.
  • Attendance: the date and time you check in, and whether the check-in was allowed or refused.
  • Notes: short staff notes about your membership (for example a freeze request you made).

We do not collect health data, biometric data or payment card details through this website.

Why we use it

  • To run your membership, take payments and issue receipts.
  • To check you in and confirm your membership is current.
  • To contact you about renewals, unpaid balances or changes to your membership.
  • To keep the records a business is normally required to keep.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Where it is stored

Member records are kept in a private Google spreadsheet that only the gym owner can open, accessed through a password-protected staff dashboard. The website itself is hosted on Vercel and contains no member data. Google and Vercel process data on our behalf under their own terms.

How long we keep it

We keep membership and payment records while your membership is active and afterwards for as long as we need them for accounting and legal reasons. When you ask us to delete your details, we remove the personal fields and keep only the minimum financial record we are required to keep.

Who can see it

Gym staff can see the details they need to check you in and take payments. Only the owner can change settings, export data or delete records. Every change is logged.

Cookies and local storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The staff dashboard stores a login token in your browser so you stay signed in, and the site remembers whether you prefer light or dark mode. Signing out clears the token.

Your choices

  • Ask what we hold about you, and get a copy.
  • Ask us to correct anything wrong.
  • Ask us to delete your details, where we are not required to keep them.
  • Ask us to stop sending renewal messages.

To do any of these, contact us at the email on our contact details or call the gym phone number. We aim to reply within 30 days.
