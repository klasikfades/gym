Terms of Membership

Last updated: —

Draft for review. These terms are a starting point, not legal advice. The gym owner should have them checked against local consumer law before launch.

1. Your membership

A membership lets you use the gym during opening hours for the period shown on your membership record. Plans, prices and duration are set by Your Gym Name and shown on the plans section of the website or at the front desk. Prices may change for future memberships; a membership already paid for is not affected.

2. Payment

  • Payment is due when a membership starts unless the gym agrees otherwise.
  • Partial payments are recorded against the membership. The balance stays due until it is paid.
  • Every payment gets a receipt number. Keep it if you need proof of payment.
  • Unpaid balances may stop a renewal or a check-in being allowed.

3. Start and end dates

Your membership starts on the date recorded at sign-up and ends the day before the same date in the final month (for example 15 January to 14 February for a one-month plan). Renewing early adds the new period after the current one ends, so no paid days are lost.

4. Freezing a membership

  • You can ask to freeze your membership for a date range agreed with staff.
  • Frozen days are added to the end of your membership.
  • You cannot check in while your membership is frozen.
  • If you come back early, the unused frozen days are returned to your end date.

5. Check-in

  • Check in at the front desk with your member ID, phone number or name.
  • Check-in is refused if your membership has expired, has not started, is frozen, or if the member record is inactive.
  • Repeat check-ins within a short window (set by the gym) count as one visit.

6. Conduct and safety

Members must follow the gym’s rules and staff instructions, use equipment as intended, and respect other members. The gym may end a membership for serious or repeated breaches. Re-rack weights, wipe down equipment and report anything damaged.

7. Health and risk

Exercise carries a risk of injury. You confirm you are fit enough to train and that you have told staff about any condition that affects your safety. If you are unsure, speak to a doctor first. The gym’s insurance and liability position should be confirmed by the owner before launch.

8. Cancellations and refunds

Cancellations and refunds are handled by the gym on a case-by-case basis under local consumer law. Contact us before cancelling so we can confirm your end date and any balance.

9. Your information

We use your details to run your membership, as described in our Privacy Policy.
